What Makes Prop Firm Operations Different

A brokerage cares about deposits, trading activity and client retention. A prop firm's concerns sit somewhere else entirely. You're selling evaluations, measuring traders against strict rules, deciding who progresses, and paying out a share of profits to the ones who succeed.

Standard broker CRMs weren't built for that cycle. They don't know what a daily loss limit is, can't move a trader from phase one to phase two, and won't tell you that three accounts are copying each other's trades. Prop firms need tooling designed around evaluation logic, and that's what this is.

What's Included

Challenge and Evaluation Setup

Single-phase, two-phase, instant funding or custom models. You define account sizes, profit targets, minimum trading days, time limits, leverage and pricing for each, and the system handles the rest.

Checkout and Account Creation

Traders choose a challenge on your website, pay through your integrated payment provider and receive their trading account automatically. No one on your team has to create logins manually.

Trader Accounts and Progression

Each trader's journey is tracked from purchase through each phase to funded status. Passing a phase can trigger the next account automatically or send it for review first, whichever your process requires.

Rule Configuration

Daily loss limits, maximum drawdown (static or trailing), profit targets, consistency rules, minimum days, news trading restrictions, weekend holding, lot size caps and more. Rules can differ between challenge types and phases.

Real-Time Rule Monitoring

The system checks trading activity against each account's rules as it happens. When a breach occurs, the account can be disabled automatically and the trader notified with the reason, so there's no argument about what happened or when.

Risk Monitoring

Dashboards flag patterns worth a closer look, such as identical trades across linked accounts, suspected copy trading, hedging between accounts, latency arbitrage behaviour or unusual concentration in a single instrument.

Performance Dashboards

Traders see their progress against targets and limits in a clear dashboard, which cuts down support questions. Your team sees firm-wide views of active challenges, pass rates, breach reasons and revenue.

Payout Review Workflow

Payout requests from funded traders arrive in a review queue with trading history, rule compliance and risk flags attached. Your team approves or queries each request, and approved payouts go out through your payment provider with a full record kept.

CRM and Admin Panel

Trader profiles, KYC status, purchase history, account history, support notes and communication logs in one place, with role-based access for support, risk and finance staff.

API Access

A documented API lets your website, affiliate system, payment providers, KYC service and trading platform exchange data with the CRM. Useful if you want a custom front end or need to plug in tools you already use.

Implementation details

Running a prop firm means watching thousands of evaluation accounts at once, each with its own target, drawdown limit and deadline. A trader breaches a rule at 3am and the account needs closing. Another passes and expects their funded account by morning. Someone requests a payout, and your team has to confirm the trading behind it was genuine. Doing that by hand doesn't scale past a few hundred traders. Our prop firm CRM and API tools automate the routine parts, from selling challenges and creating accounts to enforcing rules, tracking performance and preparing payouts for review.
