04

Technology Required

MT5 white label, trading server hosting, liquidity bridge, forex CRM, client portal, payment service provider integration and KYC integration.

Implementation

08

Result

Regional Broker: CRM Migration

01

Project Overview

An established broker whose CRM no longer kept up with its client base or partner network.

02

Client Requirement

Move to a CRM with proper MT5 integration, automated IB commissions and better reporting.

03

Business Challenge

Years of client records, documents and transaction history had to move across without interrupting live operations or confusing clients.

04

Technology Required

Forex CRM, MT5 integration, data migration, IB management and client portal.

05

Our Solution

We mapped the existing data structure, cleaned inconsistent records and set up the new CRM to run in parallel with the old one during the transition.

06

Implementation

Migration ran in phases. Each phase was checked against the old system before moving on, and the final switchover was scheduled outside busy trading hours.

07

Integrations

MT5, payment providers, KYC provider and multi-level IB commission plans.

08

Result

Operations moved to the new CRM with client history intact. IB commissions now calculate automatically, removing a monthly spreadsheet process.

03 / PORTFOLIO

Multi-Asset Broker: Liquidity Aggregation

01

Project Overview

A broker expanding beyond forex into indices and commodities.

02

Client Requirement

Connect additional liquidity providers, aggregate pricing and add backup routing.

03

Business Challenge

Each provider used different symbol names, contract sizes and price digits, and the broker had experienced pricing issues with a previous integration.

04

Technology Required

Liquidity bridge, aggregation, symbol mapping, FIX API connectivity and monitoring.

05

Our Solution

We rebuilt the symbol mapping from scratch, documented every instrument and configured routing rules to match the broker's hybrid execution model.

06

Implementation

Each provider was connected and tested on its own, then together. Price feeds were validated against provider data and failover was tested deliberately.

07

Integrations

MT5, liquidity bridge, FIX sessions and several liquidity providers.

08

Result

The broker expanded its instrument range with a documented, tested mapping and a fallback path if any single provider connection drops.

Prop Trading Firm: Challenge Automation & CRM

01

Project Overview

A growing prop firm whose team was checking rules and creating accounts largely by hand.

02

Client Requirement

Automated account creation, rule monitoring, phase progression and payout workflows.

03

Business Challenge

Rising trader numbers meant manual processes were creating delays, inconsistent rule decisions and support complaints.

04

Technology Required

Prop firm CRM, platform API integration, trader dashboard, payment integration and affiliate tracking.

05

Our Solution

We configured challenge types and rules in the CRM and connected it to the trading platform so accounts are created, monitored and updated automatically.

06

Implementation

Every stage of the trader lifecycle was tested, including breaches, passes, resets and payout reviews, before the old process was retired.

07

Integrations

Trading platform API, checkout and payment providers, KYC provider and affiliate system.

08

Result

Rules are now applied consistently with a clear record behind each decision, and traders can see their own progress, which reduced support questions.

Existing MT4 Broker: Hosting Migration & Disaster Recovery

01

Project Overview

An MT4 broker experiencing instability with its existing hosting.

02

Client Requirement

Move to more reliable infrastructure with proper monitoring and a recovery plan.

03

Business Challenge

The broker had no tested backups and no documented plan for a server failure, and clients had noticed disruption during busy sessions.

04

Technology Required

MT4 server hosting, monitoring, backup systems and disaster recovery planning.

05

Our Solution

We planned new infrastructure closer to the broker's liquidity providers, with monitoring, scheduled backups and a standby environment.

06

Implementation

The migration was rehearsed in advance and carried out during a quiet period. Backup restores were tested after the move.

07

Integrations

MT4 server, liquidity bridge and CRM.

08

Result

The broker now has monitored infrastructure, verified backups and a documented recovery procedure their team understands.
