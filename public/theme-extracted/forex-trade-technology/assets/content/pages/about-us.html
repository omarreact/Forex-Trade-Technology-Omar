01 / ABOUT

Who We Are

We're a forex technology company that builds and connects the systems brokerages run on. Trading platforms, CRM, liquidity connectivity, hosting, payments, onboarding tools and the integrations between them.

We started with a simple observation. Most brokerages don't struggle because they lack software. They struggle because their software doesn't work together. A platform from one vendor, a CRM from another, payments bolted on later and nobody responsible for the gaps in between. We set out to be the team that takes responsibility for those gaps.

It's worth being clear about what we're not. We're not a broker. We don't offer trading services, investment advice or liquidity, and we don't hold or manage client funds. Our work is technology, and we keep it that way.

02 / ABOUT

Our Expertise

Our team works across the full brokerage stack. That includes MT5 and MT4 configuration and administration, CRM development and integration, liquidity bridges, FIX API connectivity and aggregation, trading server hosting and infrastructure, payment and KYC/AML integrations, Manager API development, mobile and web trading, prop firm systems and PAMM and MAM solutions.

That range matters more than any single skill. When a client's deposit doesn't show up in their trading balance, the cause could sit in the payment provider, the webhook handling, the CRM workflow or the platform integration. A team that understands every layer can find the real problem quickly instead of passing it along.

Our Technology Approach

01

We Plan the Whole System

Even when a broker only needs one piece from us, we look at how it fits with everything else. A hosting decision affects liquidity. A CRM decision affects onboarding. Payment choices affect reporting. Planning with the full picture avoids expensive rework later.

02

We Prefer Reliable Over Flashy

Well-configured, well-documented systems that your team can understand and work with. Clever features matter less than a setup that behaves predictably on a busy trading day.

03

We Test What Could Go Wrong

Failed payments, rejected documents, dropped connections and execution rejections all happen in real operations. We test for them before launch, not after.

04

We Build for the Next Stage

Your setup should handle more clients, new products and new regions without a rebuild. We design with that growth in mind from the start.
