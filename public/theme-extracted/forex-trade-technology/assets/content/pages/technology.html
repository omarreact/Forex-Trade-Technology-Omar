How a Brokerage Technology Stack Fits Together

It helps to see the stack as a path that clients and orders travel along.

Client → Broker Website → Forex CRM (KYC, Payments, IB) → MT5 / MT4 → Liquidity Bridge → Liquidity Providers

A trader finds your brokerage through your website and registers. The CRM manages their profile, verification, funding and any IB relationship. Once approved and funded, they trade on MT5 or MT4. Their orders pass through a liquidity bridge, or a FIX connection, to your liquidity providers, and prices flow back the other way.

Around that path sits the infrastructure that keeps it running: servers, monitoring, security and backup systems. If any link in the chain is weak, clients feel it. That's why we think about the stack as one system, even when a broker only needs one part of it from us.

The Four Layers of Brokerage Technology

01

The Trading Layer

MT5 and MT4, plus the trading servers they run on. This is where client accounts, positions, orders, groups and trading conditions live. Getting configuration right here affects everything from margin calls to how prices display.

02

The Connectivity Layer

Liquidity bridges, FIX API connections, aggregation and order routing. This layer connects your trading environment to the market and puts your execution model into practice. Small configuration mistakes here can cause large problems, which is why testing matters so much.

03

The Operations Layer

CRM, payment integration and KYC/AML integration. This is where your team actually runs the business: onboarding clients, approving deposits and withdrawals, managing IBs and producing reports. The quality of integration between these systems and the trading layer decides how much manual work your staff do every day.

04

The Foundation Layer

Hosting, security, monitoring and backup. Clients rarely see this layer, but they feel it immediately when it fails. A brokerage is only as reliable as its foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What technology does a forex brokerage need?

At minimum, a trading platform, trading servers, liquidity connectivity, a CRM, payment integration, KYC tools, a website and client portal, plus hosting, security, monitoring and backups.

Do I need to use all of your technology?

No. We can provide the full stack or integrate with tools you already use.

Do you support both MT4 and MT5?

Yes. We work with both, including setups where they run side by side.

What's the difference between a liquidity bridge and FIX API?

A bridge is software that connects your platform to providers and manages routing. FIX is a messaging protocol used for direct connections. Many setups use both.

Can you review my existing technology setup?

Yes. We can assess your current stack, identify weak points and recommend improvements.
