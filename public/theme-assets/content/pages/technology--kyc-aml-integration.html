01 / ONBOARDING & VERIFICATION

Why Brokers Need This Done Properly

Most brokerages start out verifying clients by hand. A passport photo arrives by email, someone eyeballs it, a utility bill follows a day later, and approval gets noted in a spreadsheet. That's manageable at twenty sign-ups a week. At two hundred, it falls apart.

Manual handling also leaves gaps. Documents get lost in inboxes. Nobody's sure who approved which client. Expired IDs go unnoticed. And when a regulator or banking partner asks to see your onboarding records, pulling together a coherent answer takes days.

Integrated verification fixes the mechanics. Your compliance people still make the calls that need human judgement, but the collecting, checking, routing and record-keeping run on rails.

03 / ONBOARDING & VERIFICATION

What We Build

The Verification Workflow

We design the path a client takes from registration to approval. What gets collected, in what order, what happens automatically and where a human reviewer steps in. Requirements can vary by country, account type or risk level, so a client in one jurisdiction might see a different flow from a client in another.

Identity and Document Verification

We connect your chosen verification provider so clients can photograph their ID, take a selfie for a liveness check and upload proof of address straight from their phone or browser. The provider checks document authenticity, compares faces and extracts data, then passes the outcome back to your systems.

Sanctions, PEP and Risk Screening

Where your providers support it, we link screening against sanctions lists, politically exposed person databases and adverse media sources. Matches get flagged for your team to investigate rather than silently blocking or passing a client.

Risk Scoring Inputs

Country of residence, screening results, source of funds answers, account type and other factors can feed a risk rating that decides whether a client sails through or lands in an enhanced review queue. The scoring rules come from your compliance policy. We wire them in.

Approval and Rejection Handling

Reviewers work from a single queue showing documents, check results and flags side by side. They can approve, reject with a reason, or ask the client for something more. Clients are told what's needed without having to email support.

CRM and Platform Sync

Verification status lives on the client profile in your CRM. Approval can automatically unlock deposits or trading account creation on MT4 or MT5, while a pending or rejected status keeps those doors closed. No one has to remember to flip a switch.

Audit Trail

Every upload, automated result, reviewer decision and status change is logged with time and user. When an auditor, bank or regulator asks how a particular client was onboarded, the answer takes minutes to retrieve.

Ongoing Checks

Verification doesn't end at approval. Expiring documents can trigger reminders to clients, and periodic rescreening can be scheduled where your provider supports it, so client records don't quietly go out of date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which verification providers can you integrate?

Most established identity verification and screening services offer APIs we can work with. We review the provider you prefer during design and confirm what's supported before building.

Can we have different checks for different countries?

Yes. The workflow can vary by country, account type or risk level, so clients only see the steps that apply to them.

Will automated checks approve clients on their own?

That's your call. Some brokers auto-approve clear cases and send only flagged ones for review. Others want a human to sign off every client. We configure whichever approach your policy requires.

Can unverified clients still deposit or trade?

Only if your rules allow it. Verification status can control access to deposits, withdrawals and trading account creation in whatever combination your policy sets.

Does this include sanctions and PEP screening?

Yes, where the provider you choose supports it. Matches are flagged for your team to review rather than decided automatically.

How are verification records stored?

Results and decisions are recorded against each client profile with a full audit log. Retention periods and storage requirements should follow your own legal obligations, and we'll set things up to match them.

Can you add this to an existing CRM?

Usually, yes. We assess your current system's integration options and connect verification to it, or recommend changes if it can't support what you need.

Will this make our brokerage compliant?

No technology alone can do that. KYC AML integration gives your compliance team the tools and records to apply your policies consistently, while the regulatory responsibility stays with your business.
