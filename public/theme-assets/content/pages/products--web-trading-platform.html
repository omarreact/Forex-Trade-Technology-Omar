What Is a Web Trading Platform?

A web trading platform, often called a web trader or WebTrader, is a version of a trading terminal that runs entirely inside a web browser. Clients can view quotes, analyse charts, place orders and manage positions without installing a desktop application or mobile app.

For brokers running MetaTrader, MT5 web trading is the most common route. MetaTrader 5 includes a browser-based terminal that connects to your trading server, so clients see the same accounts, positions and prices they'd see on desktop or mobile. MetaTrader is developed by MetaQuotes, and we provide the brokerage technology and integration work around it.

Some brokers also want a more customised web trading experience that sits within their own branded client area. We can talk through what's realistic for your platform and budget.

How It Fits Into Your Technology Stack

Web trading doesn't work in isolation. It's one access point to a larger system.

Your trading server holds client accounts, positions and orders. The web platform connects to that server, just as the desktop and mobile terminals do. Your liquidity bridge provides the prices and routes orders, regardless of which terminal they come from. Your CRM manages the client, their verification status and their funding, and your client portal handles deposits, withdrawals and documents.

A typical client journey might look like this. A trader registers on your website, gets verified through the portal, makes a deposit and clicks "Trade Now," which opens the web platform with their account ready. No download, no setup, no searching for a server name.

Security

Browser-based trading needs to be secure without becoming awkward to use. Connections between the browser and your trading infrastructure are encrypted, login is protected, and sessions are handled sensibly so inactive sessions don't stay open forever.

We also recommend clients are reminded of basic precautions, such as logging out on shared computers. Security is a shared responsibility, and clear guidance on your website helps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do clients need to install anything?

No. The web trading platform runs in a modern web browser.

Is MT5 web trading the same as the desktop terminal?

It connects to the same trading server and accounts, but it's a lighter version. Most everyday trading tasks are covered, while advanced features like automated Expert Advisors are generally used on the desktop terminal.

Which browsers are supported?

Modern versions of the major browsers are supported. We test across them during setup.

Will trades placed on the web show up on mobile and desktop?

Yes. All terminals connect to the same trading server, so positions and orders appear everywhere.

Can the web platform carry my brokerage's branding?

Yes. Web trading is presented under your brand and linked from your website and client portal.

Can I offer web trading with MT4?

MT4 also has web trading options. We'll confirm what suits your setup during the review.
