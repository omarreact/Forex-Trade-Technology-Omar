Why Payments Are Harder for Brokers

Online shops take a payment and ship a product. Brokers deal with something messier. Money arrives in many currencies, through many methods, from clients in many countries. It has to land in the correct trading account, often within minutes. Then, unlike most businesses, you also have to send money back out, regularly and securely, after checking it's going to the right person.

On top of that, forex is treated as higher risk by many payment companies, so brokers often end up juggling several providers. Each one has its own API, its own status codes, its own settlement timing and its own quirks. Without proper integration, the result is manual work, delays and errors that are painful to unpick later.

What's Included

Payment Architecture

We plan how your payment providers fit together. Which methods serve which countries, which currencies each supports, where card payments go versus bank transfers or e-wallets, and how everything reports back into a single view. Good structure here saves a lot of patching later.

Deposit Workflow

A client chooses an amount and method in your client portal, completes payment on the provider's secure page, and once the provider confirms success, the funds appear on their trading account. Failed or abandoned payments are recorded too, so your support team can see exactly what happened when a client gets in touch.

Withdrawal Workflow

Withdrawal requests move into a review queue rather than going out automatically. Your finance team can check verification status, open positions, available margin, previous deposit methods and any rules you apply, such as returning funds to the original source first. Once approved, the payout is sent through the relevant provider and the client's balance updates.

Webhooks and Status Handling

Providers send real-time notifications when a payment succeeds, fails, is reversed or is still pending. We set your systems up to listen for these messages, verify they're genuine and act on them correctly, including handling duplicates and delayed callbacks without double-crediting anyone.

Reconciliation

Daily matching between what providers report, what your CRM recorded and what landed on trading accounts. Mismatches are flagged instead of quietly piling up until month end.

CRM and Balance Synchronisation

Every deposit and withdrawal is logged against the client profile in your CRM and reflected on their MT4 or MT5 account. Your finance, support and compliance teams all look at the same numbers.

Multiple Providers and Backup Routes

Payment providers sometimes go down, change their risk appetite or exit a market altogether. With more than one provider connected for key methods, you can route traffic elsewhere if one stops working. Routing can also follow country, currency or amount rules.

Security Setup

Card details are handled on the provider's own secure pages rather than passing through your servers, which keeps sensitive data out of your systems and reduces your exposure. API credentials are stored securely, callback endpoints are protected, and staff access to payment functions is limited by role.

How the Payment Flow Connects

Client Portal → Deposit Request → Payment Provider Secure Page → Provider Confirmation via Webhook → CRM Transaction Record → MT4 / MT5 Balance Update

Client Portal → Withdrawal Request → Finance Approval Queue → Payout via Provider → CRM Record → MT4 / MT5 Balance Update

All Transactions → Daily Reconciliation Report

Deposits flow from the portal through the provider and back as a confirmed transaction that credits the trading account. Withdrawals travel the other way, pausing for approval before payout. Every movement feeds the reconciliation report so nothing slips through unnoticed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide a forex payment gateway yourselves?

No. We integrate the payment providers you hold agreements with. The providers process the money, and we build the technical connections around them.

Can you help us find payment providers that accept forex brokers?

We can explain the technical considerations worth checking, such as supported methods, APIs, webhooks and settlement reporting. Commercial approval and terms are between you and each provider.

How quickly do deposits reach trading accounts?

Once the provider confirms a successful payment, the balance can update automatically. Overall timing depends on the payment method and the provider, since some methods confirm almost instantly while bank transfers can take longer.

Can withdrawals be fully automatic?

They can, but most brokers prefer an approval step for checks like verification status, open positions and source-of-funds rules. We set it up however your policy requires.

What happens if a payment provider goes offline?

If you have a backup provider connected for that method, routing can switch so clients can still deposit. Without one, that method is unavailable until the provider recovers.

Do card details pass through our servers?

In a standard setup, no. Clients enter card details on the provider's secure page, which keeps that data out of your systems.

Can you connect crypto payment options?

Where you've agreed terms with a crypto payment provider that offers an API, yes. Regulatory treatment of crypto payments differs by jurisdiction, so check your obligations first.

How does reconciliation work?

Provider reports, CRM records and trading account balances are matched daily. Anything that doesn't line up is flagged so your finance team can investigate. This is one of the main benefits of a proper forex payment gateway integration.
