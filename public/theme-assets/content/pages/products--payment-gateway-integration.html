Why Payments Are Harder for Brokers

Taking payments sounds like a solved problem. For forex brokers, it isn't.

Many payment providers treat trading as a higher-risk business category, which narrows the list of who will work with you. Clients come from different countries with different preferred payment methods, from cards and bank transfers to e-wallets and local options. Withdrawals need checks before money leaves. Every transaction has to match up with a trading account balance, and your finance team needs to be able to prove it.

On top of that, a single provider rarely covers everything. Most brokers end up working with several, and keeping them all connected and consistent is where problems usually start.

03 / PRODUCT

Deposit Workflow

A well-built deposit flow feels simple to the client and stays tightly controlled behind the scenes.

The client logs into your portal or app, chooses a trading account, selects a payment method suitable for their region and enters an amount. They're taken to the provider's secure payment page, or a secure embedded form, to complete the payment.

Once the provider confirms the payment, the confirmation reaches your system automatically. The transaction is recorded in the CRM, and depending on your rules, the trading account balance is updated straight away or after a review. The client sees the funds and gets a notification.

If a payment fails or is declined, the client is told clearly and can try another method. Your team can see what happened in the transaction record.

04 / PRODUCT

Withdrawal Workflow

Withdrawals need more care, because money is leaving your business.

The client requests a withdrawal from the portal, choosing a method and amount. The request goes into a review queue in your CRM. Your finance team checks it against your rules, which might include verification status, available balance, recent trading activity and whether the withdrawal method matches how the client deposited.

Once approved, the withdrawal is processed through the relevant provider, either automatically through the integration or manually by your team, depending on the provider and your preference. The trading balance is updated, the transaction is recorded and the client is notified.

Every step is logged, so if a client asks where their money is, your team can answer in seconds.

Multiple Providers and Payment Methods

01

Local Payment Methods

Clients are far more likely to deposit when they can use the methods they already trust. Supporting regional bank transfers, local e-wallets and popular local options can make a real difference in markets where cards aren't the default.

02

Multiple Providers

Connecting several providers lets you cover more regions and methods, and gives you alternatives if one provider has issues or changes its terms.

03

Smart Routing

Payments can be routed to different providers based on rules such as client country, currency, payment method or amount. If a transaction is declined by one provider, it can be retried through another where that's supported and appropriate.

04

Currency Handling

Deposits and withdrawals in different currencies are recorded clearly, with conversion details shown where relevant, so balances and reports stay accurate.

06 / PRODUCT

Webhooks and Real-Time Updates

Most payment providers send confirmations through webhooks, automatic messages sent the moment a transaction's status changes. Your system needs to receive those messages, verify they genuinely came from the provider and act on them correctly.

We build that layer carefully. Incoming notifications are authenticated, duplicates are handled so a payment isn't credited twice, and failed messages are retried or flagged. This is the detail that decides whether balances update reliably or whether your finance team spends their mornings chasing missing deposits.

07 / PRODUCT

Reconciliation

At the end of the day, the numbers in your payment providers, your CRM and your trading platform need to match. When they don't, someone has to find out why.

The integration records each transaction with its provider reference, status, amount, currency and timestamps, so matching records across systems becomes straightforward. Reports can show deposits and withdrawals by provider, method, country and period. Exceptions, such as payments confirmed by a provider but not credited, or credited but not confirmed, can be flagged for review.

Good reconciliation doesn't just save time. It's how you catch problems early and show auditors that your money flows are under control.

08 / PRODUCT

CRM and Balance Synchronisation

Payments only make sense in context. The integration ties every transaction to the right client and trading account in your CRM, and through your platform connection, to the right balance on MT5 or MT4.

That means one place to see a client's full financial history, approval workflows your finance team controls, automatic balance updates when rules allow, and clear records of who approved what and when.

09 / PRODUCT

Security and Compliance Boundaries

Payment data is sensitive, so the integration is designed to handle as little of it as possible.

Card details are entered on the provider's secure pages or secure forms, not stored on your systems. Connections to providers are encrypted, credentials are stored securely and access to payment functions in the CRM is restricted by role. Every action is logged.

It's also worth being clear about responsibilities. Your payment providers handle payment processing and their own compliance obligations. Your brokerage remains responsible for anti-money laundering checks, client verification and any regulatory requirements that apply to your business. The technology supports those processes with workflows, records and controls, but it doesn't replace them.
