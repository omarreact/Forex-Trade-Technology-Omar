03 / FOREX TRADE TECHNOLOGY

MT5 and MT4 Technology for Brokers

MetaTrader is still the platform most traders recognise, and for many brokers it's the obvious starting point. The harder question is how you get access to it and how much of it you want to control.

A white label setup gives you a branded MT5 environment with wider control over groups, symbols and trading conditions. A grey label setup runs under another provider's infrastructure, which lowers the entry cost and the technical load. We help you work out which model fits your stage, budget and plans, then configure it properly.

For brokers already running MT4, we can also support configuration, maintenance and integration work around the existing setup.

A Forex CRM Built Around How Brokerages Actually Work

Your CRM is where the business really runs. Leads come in, clients get verified, accounts get funded, IBs get paid. If those steps live in spreadsheets and email threads, growth gets messy quickly.

Our forex CRM connects lead management, client onboarding, KYC, deposits and withdrawals, trading accounts, IB commissions and reporting. It integrates with MT5, payment providers and verification tools, so your team isn't re-entering the same data in three places.

Liquidity and Connectivity

A trading platform without a reliable liquidity connection is just software. We handle the technical side of connecting your platform to liquidity providers, including bridge setup, symbol mapping, order routing and testing before anything goes live.

Where it makes sense, we can set up aggregation across multiple providers and backup connections so a single point of failure doesn't take your pricing down.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a forex broker technology provider do?

We supply and connect the systems a brokerage runs on: trading platform access, CRM, liquidity connectivity, hosting, payments, KYC tools and the integrations between them. We're a technology company, not a broker, and we don't hold or manage client funds.

Can you help if I'm starting from nothing?

Yes. A lot of our work is with new brokerages. We help you plan the stack, choose between white label and grey label, and set everything up in the right order.

I already have a platform. Can you just provide the CRM or liquidity setup?

Of course. You don't need to take the full stack. We regularly integrate with platforms and tools brokers already use.

What's the difference between MT5 white label and grey label?

A white label generally gives you more control over your trading environment and branding. A grey label runs under another provider's infrastructure and arrangement, which is cheaper to start but more limited. The right choice depends on your budget, jurisdiction and growth plans.

Do you help with licensing?

We provide technology, not regulatory services. Licensing requirements vary by jurisdiction, and we'd always recommend working with a qualified legal or compliance advisor for that side.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the scope. A grey label setup with a standard CRM moves faster than a full white label with custom integrations. We'll give you a realistic timeline after the consultation.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing technical support is part of how we work, covering monitoring, configuration changes and troubleshooting.
