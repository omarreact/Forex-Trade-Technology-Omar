What Is an MT4 Grey Label?

An MT4 grey label lets you run your own branded brokerage within another provider's MetaTrader 4 infrastructure and under that provider's licensing arrangement. You have your own section of the platform with your branding, account types and trading conditions. The host manages the underlying server environment.

It sits between two other models. Compared with an introducing broker, you get your own brand and your own clients rather than referring traders elsewhere. Compared with an MT4 white label, you get lower costs and less to manage, but also less control.

For brokers serving MT4-loyal traders on a limited budget, that balance often makes sense.

02 / SOLUTION

Why MT4 Grey Label Still Has a Place

MT4 remains the platform of choice for a large group of forex traders. Many rely on Expert Advisors and indicators written in MQL4 and don't want to rebuild them for MT5. In some regions, MT4 is still what traders expect a broker to offer.

At the same time, MetaQuotes now focuses on MT5, and new MT4 setups are more limited than they once were. A grey label can be a practical way to offer MT4 under your own brand, because you're working inside an environment that already exists. Availability still depends on the provider arrangement, and we'll confirm what's realistic before you commit to anything.

How It Works

Your brokerage is allocated its own space within the host MT4 environment. Inside it, we set up your branding, trading groups, account types and conditions within the limits of the host arrangement.

A forex CRM connects to your section of the platform, so your team can manage registrations, verification, trading accounts, deposits, withdrawals and IBs. Payment providers and KYC tools link into the CRM, and your website and client portal sit in front, bringing clients in and giving them access to MT4 under your name.

The host takes care of the core server infrastructure. You take care of the business.

MT4 Grey Label vs White Label

A white label generally gives you greater control over the platform and its configuration, with higher costs and more responsibility. A grey label operates under another provider's infrastructure and licensing arrangement, with lower entry costs, less control and more reliance on the host.

If you expect strong volumes or need deeper customisation, our MT4 white label solution may suit you better. If you want a lean start, grey label is often the sensible first step.

08 / SOLUTION

MT4 or MT5 Grey Label?

If your clients are committed MT4 traders, an MT4 grey label makes sense. If you're targeting a broader audience or want room for multi-asset trading, an MT5 grey label is usually the better long-term choice. Some brokers start with one and add the other later.
