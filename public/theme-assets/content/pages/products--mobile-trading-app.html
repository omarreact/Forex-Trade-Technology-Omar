Branded Mobile Trading App for Forex Brokers

Watch how traders actually use their accounts and you'll see it quickly. They check positions on the train, react to a price alert over lunch, top up their margin from the sofa at night. For a lot of your clients, the phone isn't a backup device. It's the main one.

A branded forex trading app puts your brokerage on that screen. Your name, your colours, your account tools and a direct connection to your trading platform and CRM, all in an app clients download from the App Store or Google Play and open every day.

Discuss Your Mobile App

01 / PRODUCT

Why a Branded App Makes a Difference

Many brokers rely entirely on the standard MetaTrader mobile apps. They work, and clients know them. But they're generic. Your brokerage becomes one server name in a list, and everything outside trading, such as deposits, withdrawals, documents and account details, happens somewhere else.

A forex broker mobile app built for your business changes that. Clients open your app, not a third-party one. They can manage their whole relationship with you in one place, from verification to funding to trading. Your brand stays in front of them every time they check a position, and you have a direct channel for notifications and updates.

That matters for retention. A client with your app on their home screen is far more likely to stay active than one who has to remember which portal to log into.

02 / PRODUCT

iOS & Android

Your app is built for both iOS and Android, so every client can use it regardless of their device. Layouts are designed for different screen sizes, from smaller phones to larger models, and tested on real devices rather than just simulators.

We also prepare the app for submission to the App Store and Google Play. Financial and trading apps face stricter review than most, and store requirements can change. We handle the technical preparation and guide you through the submission process, but final approval sits with Apple and Google, and the developer accounts are held in your brokerage's name.

03 / PRODUCT

Branded Interface

The app is designed around your brand identity: logo, colours, typography and tone. More importantly, it's designed around how traders actually use their phones. Short sessions, one-handed use, quick access to positions and balances, and nothing buried three menus deep.

We keep the interface clean. Traders need to see their numbers clearly and act quickly, especially when markets move. Decorative elements that slow them down get left out.

04 / PRODUCT

Trader Login

Clients sign in with their brokerage credentials. Biometric login using fingerprint or face recognition can be supported where the device allows it, which makes frequent logins faster without weakening security. Session handling is designed so clients aren't logged out constantly, but inactive sessions don't stay open indefinitely either.

Clients with more than one trading account can switch between them without signing out.

05 / PRODUCT

Account Management

Beyond trading, the app gives clients access to the account tasks they'd otherwise do in the client portal.

They can view balances, equity and margin across their accounts, open new trading accounts, check transaction history, start a deposit, request a withdrawal and update personal details. Each of these actions connects back to your CRM, so your team sees the same information the client does.

For your support team, this means fewer messages asking "where's my deposit?" or "how do I open another account?" because clients can find the answer themselves.

06 / PRODUCT

Trading Integration

The app connects to your trading platform so clients can trade and monitor positions from their phone. Depending on your setup, that can include live quotes, charts, order placement, open positions, pending orders and trade history.

How trading is integrated depends on your platform and the approach that suits your business. Some brokers want full trading inside their branded app. Others prefer the app to handle account management while linking to the MetaTrader mobile app for trading. We'll talk through the options and what each one involves during planning.

07 / PRODUCT

Notifications

Push notifications give you a direct line to active clients, used carefully. Clients can receive alerts for completed deposits, approved withdrawals, verification updates, margin levels and price movements they've chosen to watch. Account-related messages can be triggered automatically from your CRM.

Notifications can also be used for announcements, such as changes to trading hours around holidays. We'd always recommend restraint here. Clients who get too many messages tend to switch them off, and then the important ones don't reach them either.

08 / PRODUCT

KYC

New clients can complete onboarding inside the app. They register, then upload identity and address documents using their phone camera, which is often easier than scanning documents on a computer. Verification status is visible in the app, and clients are told clearly if anything else is needed.

Uploaded documents go straight into your CRM and any connected verification provider. Your compliance team reviews and approves as usual. The responsibility for compliance decisions stays with your business, but making the process simple on mobile means more clients actually finish it.

09 / PRODUCT

CRM Integration

The app and CRM work as one system. Registrations from the app appear in the CRM with their source recorded. Documents, deposits, withdrawal requests and account changes flow straight through. Status updates made by your team in the CRM show up in the app.

This means there's one version of every client record, whether they used the app, the website or the portal. Your team doesn't need separate processes for mobile clients.

10 / PRODUCT

Security

A trading app handles personal data, financial information and access to client funds, so security is built in from the beginning rather than added at the end.

That includes encrypted data transmission, secure authentication, protected session management, safe handling of stored data on the device and secure communication with your backend systems. The app is tested for common vulnerabilities before release, and security updates continue after launch as operating systems and threats change.
